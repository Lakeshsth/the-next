Questions students ask

Are my passport and certificates safe?

Yes. This toolkit works inside your browser. Your files are not uploaded to any server, so nobody else can see them.

Which size limit should I choose?

Use the number shown next to the upload box on your portal or in your counsellor's instructions. If it only says "under 5 MB", choose 4 MB or 5 MB.

Why can't I select text in my compressed PDF?

To shrink scanned pages, each page is saved as an image. That keeps the look but removes selectable text. If a portal needs searchable text, keep a copy of the original.

It says the file cannot reach my limit. What now?

The file has too many pages or too much detail for that size. Split it into parts (for example, one PDF per document), remove blank pages, or ask whether a higher limit is allowed.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open this page in your phone browser, choose a file from your gallery or files app, and download the result.
